Light

Inverse square law: double the distance from a small source and you get a quarter of the light, 2 stops less. Move a light further away and the subject and background become more even; move it closer and the background falls off into darkness.

Source

Softness depends on the source size relative to the subject and its distance. A 60cm softbox close up is soft; the same box across the room is hard. The sun is huge but so far away it is a hard source; clouds turn the whole sky into one big soft source.
